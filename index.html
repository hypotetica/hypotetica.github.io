Terms of Service

Effective November 3, 2026

What the tool is

Hypotetica Publisher is a desktop application that runs on the Hypotetica creator's own computer. It uploads videos produced by Hypotetica to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. The creator then reviews, edits and publishes each draft inside the TikTok app.

Who may use it

The tool is operated only by the Hypotetica creator for the Hypotetica account. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Content

Only original content created by Hypotetica is uploaded. The creator is responsible for every video and caption and must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

TikTok

Hypotetica Publisher is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok. Access to TikTok goes through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, and can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without warranties of any kind. Hypotetica may change or discontinue it at any time.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Data received from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the authorizing account (open ID, display name, avatar), through the user.info.basic scope, used to confirm that uploads go to the right account.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload drafts through the video.upload scope.

How it is stored

All data stays on the creator's own computer in a local file. Nothing is sent to servers run by Hypotetica, because there are none. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties. The only exchange is with TikTok itself, to upload the videos.

Other people's data

The tool does not access viewers, followers, comments, messages or any account other than the authorizing one.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and are deleted from the computer when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings under security and permissions, which immediately stops the tool from uploading.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes and contact
